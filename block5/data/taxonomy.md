# Sentinel-AI Intent Taxonomy

## 1. Delivery Issues

**Definition:** Problems with an order being late, missing, not delivered, or incorrectly marked as delivered.

**Example:**
> Order not received, while it is showing delivered on 19 nov, no resolution by cust. care.

---

## 2. Order Issues

**Definition:** Problems with placing, changing, cancelling, or managing an order.

**Example:**
> I would like to cancel one of the 2 orders I placed before my account was frozen.

---

## 3. Product Issues

**Definition:** Problems involving damaged, defective, incorrect, or unexpected products.

**Example:**
> Recieved_stone in place of laptop.. Need immediate action.

---

## 4. Payment & Refunds

**Definition:** Problems involving charges, failed transactions, refunds, cashback, or other payment-related issues.

**Example:**
> want to order Diwali gifts but my transactions keep getting cancelled

---

## 5. Account & Access

**Definition:** Problems with logging in, passwords, account access, account restrictions, or account information.

**Example:**
> I can't log in to that link. It says my password is incorrect.

---

## 6. Subscription & Digital Services

**Definition:** Problems involving Prime, Kindle, digital content, subscriptions, apps, or other digital services.

**Example:**
> My one month subscription of kindle unlimited has expired. But still it allows me to borrow books.

---

## 7. Severe Escalation

**Definition:** Serious safety, security, criminal, or highly urgent situations that require immediate human intervention.

**Example:**
> your delivery guy in Lincoln park NJ took my friends puppy. Need help now!!! Police next call...
