When labeling the customer messages, I looked at what each person was actually asking for and sorted them into the categories we use for Sentinel-AI. If their package was late, missing, or went to the wrong place, I marked it as a "Delivery Issue". Anything about placing, changing, or cancelling an order went under "Order Issues". If the item they got was broken, wrong, or not what they expected, it became a "Product Issue". Any money problems—like bad charges, missing refunds, or payment errors—went to "Payment & Refunds". "Account & Access" was for people who couldn't log in or forgot their passwords, and "Subscription & Digital Services" covered things like Prime, Kindle, and other digital content.

I also had to decide if a message needed to be escalated. If a customer described something really serious, urgent, or sketchy—like a safety issue or possible fraud that requires human intervention—I marked `gold_escalate` as TRUE and quickly noted the reason. If it was just a standard complaint that didn't need special attention, I marked it FALSE, left the reason blank, and just picked the best category for their main problem.
